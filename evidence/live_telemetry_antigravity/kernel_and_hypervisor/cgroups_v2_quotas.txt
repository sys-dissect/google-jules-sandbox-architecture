[Finding]
Neither memory nor CPU bandwidth throttling is imposed inside the guest cgroups v2 hierarchy.
All limits are set to 'max', confirming Google delegates 100% of resource quotas and isolation
to the host Firecracker cgroup / KVM boundary rather than constraining guest slices.
